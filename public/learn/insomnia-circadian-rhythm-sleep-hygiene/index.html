Insomnia, Circadian Drift & Nighttime Overthinking

Circadian Light Hygiene, Cognitive Offloading & Restorative Art

Sleeplessness is rarely about not being tired—it is usually about a nervous system that still believes it needs to stay on guard.

Hyperarousal Insomnia Daytime stress carries into the evening as cognitive vigilance, making the bed feel like a place for rumination rather than surrender.
Protecting Circadian Rhythm Morning sunlight exposure sets melatonin timing, while dimming 450nm blue light in the evening preserves sleep pressure.
Bedside Wind-Down Ritual Dock your device in StandBy mode, offload tomorrow's worries into a 3-minute journal, and run 4-7-8 breathing cycles.
Clinical CBT-I Solutions Chronic sleep difficulties lasting months respond strongly to Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I).
💡 Did You Know? Even a quick glance at work notifications at midnight shifts your nervous system out of parasympathetic mode, delaying deep sleep stages.
