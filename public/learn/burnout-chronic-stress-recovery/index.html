Chronic Stress & High-Performance Burnout Recovery

Multi-Week Trajectory Analytics, Micro-Recovery & Pacing

Burnout does not arrive overnight. It builds quietly through months of unrecovered micro-stress—until exhaustion, cynicism, and brain fog become your baseline.

The Three Dimensions Emotional exhaustion, cynicism/depersonalization, and a reduced sense of personal efficacy define occupational burnout.
Trajectory Drift Analytics Tracking 5-state mood check-ins and step telemetry with cubic spline modeling exposes downward trends weeks before a crash.
Micro-Recovery Throughout Work Short 90-second breathing breaks and brief walks protect cognitive stamina far better than waiting for a weekend catch-up.
Rebuilding Boundaries Work with licensed somatic clinicians to dismantle chronic overwork and establish sustainable emotional boundaries.
💡 Did You Know? Putting your emotional state into words (affect labeling) calms amygdala reactivity and re-engages rational prefrontal circuits.
